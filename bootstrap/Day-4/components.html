Vintage

Vintage Volkswagens, particularly the Beetle, are iconic for their simple, reliable design and global appeal. They are categorized into different eras based on features like the split or single-piece rear window and engine size, with the earliest split-window models being the rarest and most valuable collector's items.

F1

F1 racing, or Formula One, is the highest class of international single-seater, open-wheel auto racing sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA). It is a team sport where drivers compete in a series of races called Grands Prix to win the World Drivers' and World Constructors' Championships.
